How it works
  1. You describe the client situation and pick an email purpose and tone.
  2. The app builds a system prompt that casts Claude as a CPA and client-communications specialist, with your chosen tone and firm-style rules: plain language instead of tax jargon, reassuring rather than alarming, a clear next step, 150–250 words, and placeholder greeting and sign-off ([Client Name], [Your Name], [Firm Name]).
  3. Your purpose and situation go to Claude as the user message, in a single call to the Anthropic Messages API made directly from your browser.
  4. Claude returns a ready-to-send email body, with no subject line, which you can copy straight into your mail client.
  5. Your API key is kept in this tab's session storage and is sent only to api.anthropic.com. It never goes to this site's server and is cleared when you close the tab.
