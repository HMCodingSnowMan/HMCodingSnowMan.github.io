FAQ
Do I have to start at Module 1?
Nope. If you already know your way around formulas and formatting, jump straight to Lookups or PivotTables. Modules 1–2 are for anyone shaky on the basics — skip them if you're not.
How long does each module take?
Around 12–14 minutes depending on how long you spend on the try-it exercises. Designed to be done in one sitting.
Is this actually interview-relevant, or just theory?
Every module is built around what shows up in real analyst job postings and screening questions — VLOOKUP vs. INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS syntax, PivotTable construction, and messy real-world data. Module 8 is dedicated entirely to applying it all under interview conditions.
Is there a quiz at the end?
Each module has 3 quizzes built in throughout, plus one hands-on try-it exercise — not just a quiz at the very end. Feedback explains the reasoning behind each answer, not just whether you got it right.
