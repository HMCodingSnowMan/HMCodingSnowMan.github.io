## So... what even is Claude?

Okay, real talk. You've probably heard words like "AI" and "chatbot" thrown around a lot lately. Maybe your coworker won't stop talking about it. Maybe you've seen it on TikTok. Either way — you're here, and that's what matters. Claude is an AI assistant made by a company called Anthropic. Think of it like a really smart text buddy you can ask literally anything — and it actually gives you useful answers. 💡 Not a robot. Not Siri. Not Google. Claude is something different — it can have a real back-and-forth conversation with you and help you think through problems. By the end of this, you'll know exactly what Claude can do, how to talk to it, and how to get the most out of it. Let's go. 🚀

## How to talk to Claude (it's easier than texting your crush)

Here's the thing most people get wrong: they treat AI like a search engine. They type three words and expect magic. That's not how this works. Claude understands full sentences. The more context you give it, the better the answer. 🚫 Weak prompt: "email" ✅ Better prompt: "Write a short professional email asking my landlord to fix the leaking faucet in my kitchen. Keep it polite but firm." Three things that always make your prompts better: 1️⃣ Tell it what you want — be specific about the task 2️⃣ Give it context — who's it for? what's the situation? 3️⃣ Tell it the format — short? long? bullet points? casual tone? You don't need to be perfect. Claude will ask follow-up questions if it needs more info. It's a conversation, not a command.

## 3 tips that'll make you a Claude power user

These three things alone will put you ahead of 90% of people using AI right now. Tip 1: Ask it to try again 🔄 If you don't love the first answer, just say "try again" or "make it more casual" or "shorter please." Claude doesn't get offended. It just adjusts. Tip 2: Tell it who you are 🙋 Context helps. "I'm a nurse explaining this to a patient" or "I'm a college student writing a paper" gives Claude way more to work with and makes the output way more relevant. Tip 3: Ask follow-up questions 🔍 Claude remembers your conversation. So if it gives you something close but not perfect, just keep the convo going.
