## So you've used ChatGPT. Cool. Now let's talk about Claude.

You already know the drill — type something, AI responds. You're past the "wait this is magic" phase. Good. That means we can skip the basics and get into what actually matters. Claude is not just another ChatGPT. Same category, very different personality, different strengths, different approach. Think of it like switching from one streaming service to another — same concept, but the content, the interface, and the vibe are all different. 💡 This module covers what makes Claude different, where it shines compared to other AIs, and how to adjust your habits to get the most out of it. If you've been using ChatGPT like a search engine that writes stuff — this is going to change how you think about AI entirely. Let's get into it.

## Claude vs ChatGPT — what's actually different?

Both are large language models. Both can write, answer questions, and help you think. They're different tools. By the end of this you'll know when to reach for Claude specifically.

## Claude actually has opinions. Here's what that means for you.

One thing that surprises ChatGPT users: Claude will push back. If you ask it to do something it's not comfortable with, it won't just silently refuse or give you a bland disclaimer. It'll tell you why, often suggest an alternative, and engage with you like an actual person. ChatGPT-style response to a risky request: G I'm sorry, I can't help with that. Claude-style response: C I'm not going to help with that specific thing, but here's what I think you might actually be trying to do — and here's how I can help with that instead. This also means Claude will: ✅ Tell you when it thinks you might be wrong ✅ Share its perspective when you ask for it ✅ Engage with nuance instead of just giving you the "safe" answer ✅ Be honest if it's uncertain about something 💡 Think of it less like a tool and more like a smart collaborator who has its own point of view.

## Claude's superpower: it can read a LOT at once

Here's something most people coming from other AIs don't know: Claude can handle extremely long conversations and documents. You can paste in an entire contract, a research paper, a 50-page report — and Claude will actually read and understand all of it, not just the first few paragraphs. 🔥 What this unlocks for you: 📋 Paste your entire resume and ask for a full rewrite 📑 Drop in a long contract and ask "what should I watch out for?" 📧 Paste a whole email thread and ask "what's the tension here?" 📚 Share a long article and ask for a summary with key takeaways With other AI tools you often have to break things up or worry about it "forgetting" earlier parts of the conversation. Claude's long context window means you can work on bigger, more complex things in one go. 💡 Pro move: start your conversation by pasting in all the context upfront, then ask your questions. You'll get dramatically better answers.

## Put it into practice

You're used to giving short prompts. Now try writing a prompt that gives Claude context AND asks for something specific. Example scenario: you want Claude to review an important email you're about to send to your boss. Write the prompt you'd use — include what the email is about and what kind of feedback you want.

## 3 ChatGPT habits to drop when using Claude

If you're bringing habits from other AI tools, some of them will hold you back. Here's what to unlearn: ❌ Habit 1: Restarting conversations constantly With Claude, staying in the same conversation is usually better. It remembers everything said earlier and uses it to give you more relevant answers over time. Don't start fresh every time — keep the thread going. ❌ Habit 2: Short, keyword-style prompts "Email for client" doesn't cut it. Claude thrives on context. The more you tell it — who's involved, what the goal is, what tone you want — the better the output. Write prompts like you're briefing a smart colleague. ❌ Habit 3: Accepting the first answer Claude is built for back and forth. If the first answer is 80% there, just say what's missing. "Make it shorter." "More formal." "Add a joke." It iterates fast and doesn't lose context between turns. ✅ The new habit: treat it like a conversation, not a command line. Brief it, react to it, redirect it. That's when Claude is at its best.
